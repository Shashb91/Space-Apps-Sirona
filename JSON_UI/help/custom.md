# Custom Point

Study a few spots closely: their ground, and what the Sun and the Earth do above them.

## Adding a point

- Type a **latitude** and a **longitude** (south and west are negative), then **Add point**, or simply click the Moon.
- Up to 4 points, all looked at for the same **date and time** (UTC).
- Each point gets a small panel on the Moon with its name, elevation and geology. Click a point to select it.

## The right panel

It shows the selected point over a number of days from the chosen date: **30** by default. Change the number in the panel's title, from 1 to 366 days.

- When the **Sun** is up and how much of its disc shows, and how much power reaches the ground.
- When the **Earth** is above the horizon: a direct radio link needs it more than 5° up.
- A summary: the **longest night** and the **longest time without an Earth link**. A night that starts before the period or ends after it is counted in full.

Over long periods, the values are taken every few hours instead of every hour.

## Good to know

- Hills and crater walls can hide the Sun and the Earth: when the server has the relief map, it takes them into account.
- The Sun and Earth values come from the team's Python server, from NASA/JPL ephemerides (SPICE). Without the server, the panel says so.
