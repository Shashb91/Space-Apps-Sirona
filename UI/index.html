Parametric Search

Criteria

Sun visible: how much of the Sun's disc shows. Earth link: Earth more than 5° up. Temperature: of the surface, from the team's thermal model (K, smooth Moon: no relief shadows). Water: H₂O in the soil, from the team's water map (ppm). Slope: mean over ~2 km. Altitude: above the 1,737.4 km sphere. Geology: the age of the surface (USGS geologic map). Terrain: how easy its geologic unit is to land on (team rating). Longest night: the longest stretch with no Sun at all; Earth link gap: the longest stretch without an Earth link (both in days, over the period; one that starts before it or ends after it counts in full). On average: each value is averaged over the period, and Earth link becomes the % of the time it is possible.
